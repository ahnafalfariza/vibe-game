Yeti, Set, Go!

You're a yeti on skis. Behind you, the whole mountain is coming down. Jump, backflip, hitch a ride on a penguin, and whatever you do, don't stop.

Bunny HillEasiest

One button. That's the whole tutorial.

  1. Spaceortap Jump. Off anything. The bigger the bump, the bigger the air.
  2. hold Backflip. Keep holding in the air and the yeti goes over. Hold longer, flip more.
  3. let go Land it. Skis flat to the snow. This is the part that takes a while.

Penguin PassMore difficult

Ski into an animal. Now you're riding it.

Rentals No deposit. No helmet.

Penguin
Glides. Flips faster than you do.
Eagle
Picks you up and flies. Hold to climb.
Snowmobile
Runs on a tank of fuel. Has room for a passenger.
Polar bear
Goes through rocks. Goes through cabins.

Anything you ride takes the hit for you. Smack a rock and you lose the ride, not the run.

Avalanche AlleyMost difficult

You can't outrun it. You can outrun it for longer.

The avalanche starts a few metres behind you and speeds up the longer you ski. Wipe out and it closes the gap. Jump the rocks, use the ice, ride the cabin roofs.

Twilight PinesExperts only

There's a second mountain. Rank 3 gets you in.

You always have three goals, like land a clean double or get picked up by an eagle. Finish all three and you rank up. Every rank multiplies your whole score by another ×0.25, and some of them come with something extra.
